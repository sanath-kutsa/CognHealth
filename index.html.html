About the model

The Alzheimer's score uses logistic regression: score = 100 / (1 + e−(b + Σ wᵢxᵢ)). Each weight is the log relative risk of a risk factor, inspired by published reviews such as the Lancet Commission on dementia prevention. Questions are adaptive: follow-ups appear only when earlier answers make them relevant.

FactorWeight

The skills estimate

Difficulty moves up after a correct answer and down after a miss (an item-response-theory style staircase). The final index comes from the difficulty you reach, on a 70–130 scale.

Limits, honestly

The weights are illustrative and not calibrated on patient data. Real research uses datasets like ADNI, OASIS and NACC plus clinical tests. The included Python file trains the same model on a synthetic cohort. The score is an awareness indicator, not a probability of getting Alzheimer's, and the cognitive index is not a real IQ.
